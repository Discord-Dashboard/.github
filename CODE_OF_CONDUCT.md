# Code of conduct

The Discord-Dashboard community follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in every repository, issue, pull request and discussion of this
organization, and in our community spaces.

## In short

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or unwelcome attention.
- Don't publish anyone's private information.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, contact a maintainer privately, on GitHub or on
our Discord server. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may
remove comments, lock threads, or block people from the organization, as the
Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
